**What happened:** The National Urban League and the Massachusetts Association of Community Development Corporations filed suit on October 29 challenging the CFPB's overhaul of Regulation B/Subpart A, which governs equal credit opportunity requirements.

**Why it matters:** The lawsuit creates legal uncertainty around the revised Regulation B framework. If the challenge succeeds, institutions that have already begun implementing the new requirements may need to revert to prior standards, creating compliance cost and operational disruption. If the rule survives, the litigation record may clarify interpretive questions. Fair lending and compliance teams should track the case and consider maintaining parallel compliance capabilities until resolution.

**Business impact:** Fair lending compliance teams should monitor the litigation and maintain flexibility to operate under either the revised or prior Regulation B framework.
